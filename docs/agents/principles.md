# Principles

> **Fill this in.** The few ideas every other rule in your framework serves. When a rule and a principle disagree, the principle wins. Keep the list short; each principle should name where it comes from.

## Questions to answer

- What must be true before a piece of work counts as done?
- Which rules must be enforced by something that fails, and which can stay as written guidance?
- Who decides what done looks like, and when: before the work starts, or during review?
- Who is allowed to judge a piece of work, and who is not?
- What happens when the same mistake shows up again?
- How much do you trust the agents today, and what has to happen before you trust them more?
